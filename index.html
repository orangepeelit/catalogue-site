HOW IT WORKS

One governed source. A simple public view.

Catalogue does not maintain a second product inventory. It projects the current portfolio from governed Orange Peel knowledge.

Public presentation is deliberately smaller than the knowledge underneath it. Internal identifiers, incomplete engineering notes and unresolved implementation detail remain available for assurance without leaking into the normal interface.
